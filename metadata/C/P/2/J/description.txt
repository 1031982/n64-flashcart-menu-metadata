The battle to be a master Trainer continues, and Pokémon Stadium 2 delivers everything, and more, that a fan would want to see in a sequel. With tougher battles, sharper-looking graphics and a stadium full of extras, Pokémon Stadium 2 is tilted in favor of fans who can play using the Transfer Pak that allows uploading Pokémon from Pokémon Gold, Silver, Yellow, Red or Blue.

Pokémon Stadium 2 bristles with new attacks, new Pokémon and new strategies. The deep academy features an outstanding series of tutorials as well as an extremely comprehensive Poké-reference library. For a lighthearted break, players can play 12 new arcade-style minigames.
